ABOUT ME

BACKGROUND

I grew up in Gothenburg, but in 2022 I decided to move to Stockholm and begin my studies at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, where I am currently in my first year of an MSc in Industrial Design Engineering.

WHAT IS COMING

After an exchange semester in Tokyo this spring, I am looking for a paid internship for Fall 2026 (Sept-Dec). I am always looking to improve my skills and I am looking forward to contributing to a real-world team.

MISSION AND VALUES

My mission is to develop products that ease everyday life while ensuring retained value and purpose over time.


I view sustainability as an essential, natural thread throughout the entire development process.


As an engineer and product developer, I thrive when analyzing complex problems from multiple perspectives to uncover innovative and meaningful solutions.
